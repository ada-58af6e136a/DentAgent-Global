ORDER PROCESS AND PRODUCTION LEAD TIMES
Last updated: 2026-05

PART 1 — PRODUCTION STAGES

The standard production lead time and turnaround time for each product type is set out in Part 2. All lead times assume receipt of a clean, complete impression or digital scan file (STL format) and a complete prescription on a standard business day.

Impression receipt: When a client's physical impression, digital scan file (STL), or prescription is received, the customer service team records the case details and confirms that the order information is complete. This includes client name, case reference, product type, tooth position, shade requirement, material, and any special instructions. Typical duration: same business day after receipt during normal working hours (Monday to Saturday, 9 am to 6 pm GMT+8).

Inspection: The technical team reviews the impression, scan, or prescription to confirm it is usable for production. They check for missing information, unclear preparation margins, incomplete bite registration, damaged impressions, missing STL files, or conflicting instructions. If the case is incomplete or unclear, customer service contacts the client within 24 hours and requests clarification or corrected files. Typical duration: same business day as receipt, or within 1 business day.

Design: Once the case is accepted, the CAD design team prepares the digital restoration design according to the prescription and material requirements. The design stage includes margin marking, occlusion adjustment, anatomy design, contact point checking, and preparation for milling or pressing. If the client requests design confirmation before production, the lead time is extended until written approval is received. Typical duration: 1 business day for standard single-unit cases; complex bridge or implant cases may require additional time.

Production: After design approval (or upon completion of design for standard cases), the case moves to the production stage. Depending on the product type, this includes milling, cutting, sintering, porcelain layering, polishing, and other laboratory steps. Full-ceramic and zirconia restorations require additional processing time for sintering and porcelain finishing. Complex bridge cases, implant abutment cases, or multi-unit cases require longer production time than single-unit restorations. See Part 2 for specific production lead times and turnaround times by product.

Quality control: Before shipping, the finished product is inspected by the quality control team. They check fit, surface finish, shade match, occlusion, contact points, and whether the final product matches the prescription. If the product does not pass internal quality control, it is adjusted or remade before dispatch. Typical duration: same business day as production completion, or within 1 business day.

Shipping: After quality approval, the order is packed and dispatched. Customer service sends a dispatch notification to the client by email, including the courier name, tracking number, dispatch date, and estimated delivery date. Typical dispatch timing: same business day as quality control approval, or the following business day.

PART 2 — LEAD TIME SUMMARY

All production lead times and turnaround times below are counted from the date we receive a clean, usable impression or STL digital scan file and a complete prescription. Lead times are in business days and exclude public holidays and weekends.

Production time and lead time — Wieland Zenostar zirconia crown (monolithic or high-translucency layered):
The standard production turnaround and lead time for a Wieland Zenostar zirconia crown is 5 to 7 business days from receipt of a clean impression or STL file. Rush turnaround for a Wieland Zenostar zirconia crown is available in 3 business days at a 30% surcharge on the unit price.

Production time and lead time — IPS e.max lithium disilicate crown (pressed or CAD-milled):
The standard production turnaround and lead time for an IPS e.max crown is 5 to 7 business days from receipt of a clean impression or STL file. Rush turnaround for an IPS e.max crown is available in 3 business days at a 30% surcharge.

Production time and lead time — PFM crown (Vita VM9 porcelain over cobalt-chromium alloy):
The standard production turnaround and lead time for a PFM crown is 5 to 7 business days from receipt of a clean impression. Rush turnaround for a PFM crown is available in 3 business days at a 30% surcharge.

Production time and lead time — PMMA provisional crown (temporary):
The standard production turnaround and lead time for a PMMA provisional crown is 2 to 3 business days from receipt of a clean impression or STL file. Rush processing is not available for PMMA provisional crowns — the 2 to 3 business day turnaround already reflects expedited production.

Production time and lead time — IPS e.max veneer, pressed lithium disilicate:
The standard production turnaround and lead time for a pressed IPS e.max veneer is 5 to 7 business days from receipt of a clean impression or STL file. Rush turnaround for an IPS e.max veneer is available in 3 business days at a 30% surcharge.

Production time and lead time — Wieland Zenostar zirconia inlay or onlay / IPS e.max inlay or onlay:
The standard production turnaround and lead time for a zirconia or IPS e.max inlay or onlay is 5 business days from receipt of a clean impression or STL file. Rush turnaround is available in 3 business days at a 30% surcharge.

Production time and lead time — Implant crown with custom-milled Wieland Zenostar zirconia abutment:
The standard production turnaround and lead time for an implant crown with a custom zirconia abutment is 7 to 10 business days from receipt of complete implant system information, STL scan data, and a complete prescription including implant brand, platform diameter, and connection type. Rush processing is not available for implant abutment cases. The additional abutment design and milling step is required and cannot be shortened without compromising fit accuracy.

Production time and lead time — Wieland Zenostar zirconia dental bridge, 3-unit:
The standard production turnaround and lead time for a 3-unit Wieland Zenostar zirconia bridge is 7 to 10 business days from receipt of a full-arch impression or STL scan file. Rush turnaround in 5 business days is available for standard 3-unit zirconia bridge cases at a 30% surcharge. Rush processing is not available for complex multi-unit bridges or bridge cases requiring client design approval before production begins.

Production time and lead time — Complete removable denture (full arch, premium acrylic):
The standard production turnaround and lead time for a full-arch complete removable denture is 10 to 14 business days from receipt of the master model and bite registration. Rush processing is not available for complete denture cases — the try-in and verification stages require the full production time and cannot be shortened. (French: délai de production pour une prothèse amovible complète. Spanish: tiempo de producción para dentadura completa removible. Chinese: 全口义齿生产周期。)

Production time and lead time — Cobalt-chromium framework removable partial denture:
The standard production turnaround and lead time for a cobalt-chromium (CoCr) framework removable partial denture is 7 to 10 business days from receipt of a full-arch impression or working model. Rush processing is not available for CoCr framework partial denture cases.

Production time and lead time — Acrylic removable partial denture (without metal framework):
The standard production turnaround and lead time for an acrylic removable partial denture without a metal framework is 7 to 10 business days from receipt of impression or model. Rush processing is not available.

Production time and lead time — Flexible partial denture (Valplast thermoplastic nylon):
The standard production turnaround and lead time for a Valplast flexible partial denture is 7 to 10 business days from receipt of the impression or model. Rush processing is not available for Valplast flexible partial cases.

Design-confirmation cases: Lead time and production turnaround begin or continue only after the client provides written confirmation of the CAD design.

Cases requiring corrected files or clarification: Lead time is counted from the date the corrected impression, STL file, prescription, or missing information is received and accepted by the technical team.

PART 3 — COMMON DELAY CAUSES

Incomplete prescription: If the prescription does not include product type, tooth position, shade, material, or special instructions, the case is paused until the client provides the missing details. The production lead time restarts from the date the complete information is received.

Unusable or unclear impression: If the impression is damaged, distorted, incomplete, or does not show the preparation margin clearly, production is delayed while the client sends a corrected impression or STL scan. The lead time begins from the date the corrected impression is received.

Missing or incorrect digital files: If the STL file, bite scan, or implant library information is missing or incompatible, the design team cannot proceed until corrected files are received.

Design approval pending: If the client requests to review and approve the CAD design before production, the production turnaround is paused until written confirmation is received.

Complex case requirements: Implant cases, multi-unit bridges, shade-sensitive anterior restorations, or cases requiring porcelain layering may require additional production and quality control time beyond the standard lead time.

Shipping and delivery delay: International delivery (livraison internationale, envío internacional, 国际配送) may be affected by courier schedules, customs clearance, public holidays, weather disruption, or local delivery conditions outside the laboratory's direct control. Shipping and delivery delays are not included in the production lead time.

PART 4 — RUSH PROCESSING AND SHIPPING

Rush orders: Rush processing is available for single-unit crowns, IPS e.max veneers, and inlay or onlay cases (3 business day turnaround) and for standard 3-unit Wieland Zenostar zirconia bridge cases (5 business day turnaround), subject to current production capacity and case completeness. Rush processing is not available for implant abutment cases, complete dentures, partial dentures (CoCr framework, acrylic, or Valplast), or PMMA provisional crowns. Rush surcharge is 30% of the standard unit price. Rush orders must be confirmed in writing by customer service before the surcharge is applied. (French: traitement urgent; Spanish: producción urgente; Chinese: 加急生产。)

Rush order limitations: Rush turnaround times begin only after all required case information — impression or STL file, prescription, bite registration, and any implant system details — has been received and accepted by the technical team. Rush orders cannot be cancelled once production has begun.

Shipping notification: When an order is dispatched, customer service notifies the client by email. The notification includes the courier name, tracking number, dispatch date, and estimated delivery date. (French: notification d'expédition; Spanish: notificación de envío; Chinese: 发货通知。)

Shipping regions and charges: Clients in the United States are charged a flat shipping fee of USD 12 per shipment via FedEx International Priority. Clients in France, Germany, the Netherlands, Spain, and Belgium are charged USD 15 to USD 25 per shipment depending on parcel weight and courier selection (DHL or FedEx). For regular clients, shipping charges are consolidated and invoiced monthly. Domestic clients in China are quoted separately in CNY.

Late delivery and shipping delay handling: If a client reports that an order has not arrived after the expected delivery date (retard de livraison / retraso en la entrega / 配送延误), customer service checks the tracking information, confirms dispatch status, contacts the courier if needed, and updates the client with the latest available information within 1 business day. If the order has not yet shipped, customer service confirms the current production stage and provides a realistic next update time. Customer service must not promise an unverified delivery date.

Safe response rule: Customer service must not invent production dates, shipping dates, rush fees, lead times, or delivery commitments that are not confirmed by the current production team. If the answer is not available in this document, the team must tell the client that they will check the case and follow up within 1 business day.
