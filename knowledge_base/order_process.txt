PART 3 — COMMON DELAY CAUSES

Incomplete prescription: If the prescription does not include product type, tooth position, shade, material, or special instructions, the case may be paused until the client provides the missing details.

Unusable or unclear impression: If the impression is damaged, distorted, incomplete, or does not show the preparation margin clearly, production may be delayed while the client sends a corrected impression or scan.

Missing or incorrect digital files: If the STL file, scan file, bite file, or implant library information is missing or incompatible, the design team may not be able to continue until corrected files are received.

Design approval pending: If the client requests to review and approve the CAD design before production, the production timeline may be extended until written confirmation is received.

Complex case requirements: Implant cases, multi-unit bridges, shade-sensitive anterior restorations, or cases requiring porcelain layering may require additional production and quality control time.

Shipping and customs delay: International delivery may be affected by courier schedules, customs clearance, public holidays, weather disruption, or local delivery issues outside the factory's direct control.
