CROWNS AND SINGLE-UNIT RESTORATIONS

Overview of crown material options and pricing: We offer four main crown and single-unit restoration options. Full-ceramic Wieland Zenostar zirconia crowns range from USD 85 to USD 130 per unit. Full-ceramic IPS e.max lithium disilicate crowns range from USD 95 to USD 145 per unit. Porcelain-fused-to-metal (PFM) crowns range from USD 55 to USD 85 per unit. PMMA provisional (temporary) crowns range from USD 35 to USD 55 per unit. See below for clinical recommendations, material properties, and lead times for each option.

Full-ceramic crown — Wieland Zenostar zirconia (monolithic or high-translucency layered): The unit price for a Wieland Zenostar zirconia crown ranges from USD 85 to USD 130 per unit, depending on the zirconia grade selected, case complexity, and total units ordered. Monolithic Wieland Zenostar zirconia — a full-contour, single-block restoration with no porcelain overlay — is priced toward the lower end of this range. It is recommended for posterior restorations, bruxism patients, and cases where strength and fracture resistance are the priority. High-translucency layered Wieland Zenostar zirconia — with hand-applied porcelain over a zirconia coping — is priced toward the upper end of this range and is recommended for anterior and premolar restorations where aesthetics are the clinical priority. A volume discount of 5% on the unit price applies to orders of 10 or more units placed in a single submission. The standard lead time is 5 to 7 business days from receipt of a clean, usable impression or digital scan file. Rush processing in 3 business days is available at a 30% surcharge on the unit price. Minimum order quantity is 1 unit.

Full-ceramic crown — IPS e.max lithium disilicate (pressed or CAD-milled): The unit price for an IPS e.max lithium disilicate crown ranges from USD 95 to USD 145 per unit. IPS e.max crowns are recommended for anterior restorations requiring the highest level of translucency and natural light transmission, particularly for upper anterior teeth where matching adjacent natural tooth structure is the priority. Both pressed and CAD-milled versions are available; pressed IPS e.max generally delivers a slightly more lifelike aesthetic result for complex anterior cases. This material is not recommended for patients with severe bruxism or for posterior restorations under heavy occlusal load. A volume discount of 5% applies to orders of 10 or more units. The standard lead time is 5 to 7 business days. Rush processing in 3 business days is available at a 30% surcharge. Minimum order quantity is 1 unit.

Porcelain-fused-to-metal crown — PFM (Vita VM9 porcelain over cobalt-chromium alloy): The unit price for a PFM crown ranges from USD 55 to USD 85 per unit. PFM crowns are a reliable, cost-effective choice for posterior restorations where strength is prioritised over maximum aesthetics. They are well suited to patients with heavy bite forces, long-span bridges, or cases where budget is a significant factor. Our standard PFM uses Vita VM9 porcelain fired over a cobalt-chromium alloy substructure. Precious metal alloy (gold-based) substructures are available on request at additional cost — please contact us for a quotation. A volume discount of 5% applies to orders of 10 or more units. The standard lead time is 5 to 7 business days from receipt of a clean impression. Rush processing in 3 business days is available at a 30% surcharge. Minimum order quantity is 1 unit.

PMMA provisional crown (polymethyl methacrylate, temporary): The unit price for a PMMA provisional crown ranges from USD 35 to USD 55 per unit. PMMA provisionals are tooth-coloured temporary restorations milled from a CAD design. They are used during implant osseointegration periods, treatment planning evaluation phases, or while the permanent restoration is being fabricated. PMMA provisionals are not intended as long-term restorations and should not be used in high-load posterior positions for extended periods. The standard lead time is 2 to 3 business days. Rush processing is not available for PMMA provisionals, as the standard 2 to 3 business day lead time already reflects expedited production. Minimum order quantity is 1 unit.

VENEERS AND CONSERVATIVE RESTORATIONS

Overview of veneer, inlay, and onlay options and pricing: We offer three main conservative restoration options. Pressed IPS e.max veneers range from USD 85 to USD 120 per unit. Wieland Zenostar zirconia inlays and onlays range from USD 65 to USD 95 per unit. IPS e.max pressed inlays and onlays range from USD 75 to USD 110 per unit. See below for clinical recommendations and lead times for each option.

IPS e.max veneer — pressed lithium disilicate: The unit price for a pressed IPS e.max veneer ranges from USD 85 to USD 120 per unit. IPS e.max pressed veneers are recommended for anterior teeth where maximum translucency and the most natural aesthetic outcome are required. They are bonded adhesively to prepared tooth surfaces, allowing more conservative preparation than a full crown. This material is not recommended for patients with parafunctional habits such as bruxism. The standard lead time is 5 to 7 business days. Rush processing in 3 business days is available at a 30% surcharge. Minimum order quantity is 1 unit.

Inlay or onlay — Wieland Zenostar zirconia: The unit price for a zirconia inlay or onlay ranges from USD 65 to USD 95 per unit, depending on the size, location, and complexity of the restoration. Zirconia inlays and onlays are recommended for posterior teeth requiring a conservative indirect restoration with excellent strength and long-term durability. They are suitable for patients with moderate to heavy bite forces. The standard lead time is 5 business days from receipt of a clean impression or digital scan. Rush processing in 3 business days is available at a 30% surcharge. Minimum order quantity is 1 unit.

Inlay or onlay — IPS e.max pressed lithium disilicate: The unit price for an IPS e.max pressed inlay or onlay ranges from USD 75 to USD 110 per unit. IPS e.max inlays and onlays are recommended for anterior and premolar teeth requiring conservative restorations with a high aesthetic standard. They are not recommended for patients with bruxism or in high-load molar positions. The standard lead time is 5 business days. Rush processing in 3 business days is available at a 30% surcharge. Minimum order quantity is 1 unit.

IMPLANT RESTORATIONS

Implant crown with custom-milled zirconia abutment — Wieland Zenostar zirconia: The price for a combined custom-milled zirconia abutment and full-ceramic crown ranges from USD 150 to USD 220 per unit. This price covers the design and milling of the custom zirconia abutment and the fabrication of the Wieland Zenostar zirconia crown as a single integrated implant restoration. To begin production, we require the implant brand name, platform diameter, connection type (internal or external hex), and tooth position. This information is essential to confirm implant library compatibility before the design stage begins. The standard lead time is 7 to 10 business days due to the additional abutment design and milling step. Rush processing is not available for implant abutment cases. Minimum order quantity is 1 unit.

BRIDGES

Zirconia dental bridge — Wieland Zenostar zirconia, 3-unit: The price for a standard 3-unit full-zirconia bridge ranges from USD 220 to USD 310 for the complete 3-unit restoration. Additional pontic units beyond the initial 3-unit span are priced at USD 70 to USD 100 per pontic unit. A volume discount of 5% on the unit price applies to bridge orders of 10 or more units in a single submission. We require a full-arch impression or digital scan file in STL format and clear indication of the abutment teeth and pontic span. Rush is not available for complex multi-unit bridges or cases requiring client design approval before production. Minimum order is 1 three-unit bridge.

REMOVABLE PROSTHETICS

Overview of removable denture options and pricing: We offer four main categories of removable prosthetics. Complete removable dentures (full-arch, premium acrylic) range from USD 180 to USD 260 per arch. Cobalt-chromium framework removable partial dentures range from USD 100 to USD 140 per unit. Acrylic removable partial dentures without a metal framework range from USD 55 to USD 80 per unit. Valplast flexible partial dentures (thermoplastic nylon) range from USD 140 to USD 200 per unit. See below for clinical recommendations, material details, and lead times for each option.

Complete removable denture — full arch, premium acrylic with acrylic prosthetic teeth: The price for a single-arch complete removable denture ranges from USD 180 to USD 260 per arch. This price includes fabrication on the working model and one set of try-in wax rims. Cases requiring significant bite registration correction or more than one try-in appointment may incur an additional charge of USD 30 to USD 50, which will be communicated to you before proceeding. The standard lead time is 10 to 14 business days from receipt of the master model and bite registration. Rush processing is not available for complete denture cases. Minimum order quantity is 1 arch.

Cobalt-chromium framework removable partial denture — cast CoCr framework with acrylic base and prosthetic teeth: The price for a cobalt-chromium framework removable partial denture ranges from USD 100 to USD 140 per unit, depending on the number of clasps, rest seats, and prosthetic teeth required. CoCr framework partial dentures provide rigid cross-arch stability, excellent durability, and precise clasp retention, making them the recommended clinical choice for patients who require long-term, reliable posterior support from a removable prosthesis. The metal clasps are visible in the mouth — for patients with aesthetic concerns about visible clasps, a Valplast flexible partial denture may be more appropriate. CoCr partials are not suitable for patients with a confirmed cobalt or chromium allergy. The standard lead time is 7 to 10 business days from receipt of a full-arch impression or working model. Rush processing is not available for partial denture cases. Minimum order quantity is 1 unit.

Acrylic removable partial denture — without metal framework: The price for an acrylic removable partial denture without a metal framework ranges from USD 55 to USD 80 per unit, depending on the number of teeth required. Acrylic partials are the most cost-effective removable prosthetic option and are best suited for short-term or transitional use. They are not recommended as a long-term replacement for posterior support, as the acrylic base has lower rigidity and durability than a cobalt-chromium framework. The standard lead time is 7 to 10 business days. Rush processing is not available. Minimum order quantity is 1 unit.

Flexible partial denture — Valplast thermoplastic nylon: The price for a Valplast flexible partial denture ranges from USD 140 to USD 200 per unit, depending on the number of teeth and clasps required. Valplast partials are recommended for patients who require a metal-free, gum-coloured clasp option and cannot receive a fixed restoration. Their flexible nylon clasps blend with the gum tissue and are nearly invisible when in place, making them the preferred aesthetic option for patients who object to visible metal clasps. Valplast partials are not suitable for cases requiring rigid cross-arch stability or extensive posterior support. Additions or relining are not possible after fabrication. The standard lead time is 7 to 10 business days. Rush processing is not available for flexible partial cases. Minimum order quantity is 1 unit.

PRICING NOTES

All prices are standard international wholesale rates in USD per unit unless otherwise stated. Prices do not include international shipping charges.

Volume discount: A 5% discount on the base unit price is applied automatically to orders of 10 or more units placed in a single submission. The discount applies to the base unit price only and does not apply to rush surcharges or shipping charges.

Rush processing: Rush production (where available) requires written confirmation from our customer service team before the surcharge is applied. We will not apply a rush surcharge without explicit client agreement. Rush orders cannot be cancelled once production has begun.

Shipping charges: Clients in the United States are charged a flat shipping fee of USD 12 per shipment via FedEx International Priority. Clients in France, Germany, the Netherlands, Spain, and Belgium are charged USD 15 to USD 25 per shipment depending on parcel weight and the courier service selected. For regular clients, shipping charges are consolidated and invoiced monthly. Domestic clients in China are quoted in CNY — please request a separate domestic price sheet.

Pre-case consultation: For cases involving unusual tooth morphology, non-standard shades, or specific patient requirements not covered in this price list, we recommend submitting a pre-case consultation request before sending the physical impression or scan file. This avoids unnecessary remakes and ensures the most accurate pricing before you commit to a case.
