Full-ceramic crown (Wieland zirconia): The unit price ranges from $85 to $130 USD depending on case complexity and the number of units ordered. Volume pricing applies for orders of 5 or more units in a single submission. This product is recommended for anterior and premolar restorations where natural aesthetics are the primary requirement. The standard lead time is 5 to 7 business days from the date we receive a clean, usable impression. Rush processing is available in 3 business days for an additional surcharge of 30%. Minimum order quantity is 1 unit.

Full-ceramic crown (IPS e.max lithium disilicate): The unit price ranges from $95 to $145 USD. IPS e.max crowns are recommended for anterior restorations requiring the highest level of translucency and light transmission, particularly in the upper anterior region. This material is not recommended for patients with severe bruxism or for posterior full-arch cases under heavy occlusal load. The standard lead time is 5 to 7 business days. Rush processing in 3 business days is available at a 30% surcharge. Minimum order quantity is 1 unit.

Porcelain-fused-to-metal crown (PFM, Vita VM9 porcelain): The unit price ranges from $55 to $85 USD. PFM crowns are a reliable, cost-effective choice for posterior restorations where strength is prioritised over maximum aesthetics. They are suitable for patients with heavy bite forces where all-ceramic restorations carry a higher fracture risk. The standard lead time is 5 to 7 business days from receipt of a clean impression. Rush processing is available in 3 business days at a 30% surcharge. Minimum order quantity is 1 unit.

Implant crown with custom zirconia abutment (Wieland zirconia): The unit price ranges from $150 to $220 USD for the combined abutment and crown. This price includes the custom-milled zirconia abutment and the full-ceramic crown as a single unit. We require the implant brand and platform details, the scan body or impression coping type, and the implant position before we can begin production. The standard lead time is 7 to 10 business days due to the additional abutment design step. Rush processing is not available for implant cases. Minimum order quantity is 1 unit.

Zirconia dental bridge (3-unit, Wieland zirconia): The price for a standard 3-unit full-zirconia bridge ranges from $220 to $310 USD for the full unit. Additional pontic units are priced at $70 to $100 USD per pontic. We require a complete arch impression or digital scan file (STL) and clear indication of the abutment teeth and pontic span. The standard lead time is 7 to 10 business days from receipt of a usable impression. Rush processing in 5 business days is available at a 30% surcharge. Minimum order is 1 three-unit bridge.

Complete removable denture (full arch, premium acrylic): The price for a full upper or lower complete denture ranges from $180 to $260 USD per arch. The price includes try-in wax rims. Cases requiring significant bite registration correction or multiple try-in appointments may incur an additional charge of $30 to $50 USD, which will be communicated before proceeding. The standard lead time is 10 to 14 business days for a full-arch complete denture. Rush processing is not available for complete denture cases. Minimum order quantity is 1 arch.

Flexible partial denture (Valplast): The price ranges from $140 to $200 USD depending on the number of teeth and clasps required. Valplast partials are recommended for patients who require a metal-free, gum-coloured clasp option and cannot receive a fixed restoration. They are not suitable as a long-term replacement for extensive posterior support. The standard lead time is 7 to 10 business days. Rush processing is not available for flexible partial cases. Minimum order quantity is 1 unit.

PRICING NOTES

Prices shown are standard international wholesale rates in USD. Orders from France, Germany, the Netherlands, Spain, and Belgium are subject to an additional international shipping charge of $15 to $25 USD per shipment, depending on parcel weight and the selected courier service. Clients in the United States are charged a flat shipping rate of $12 USD per shipment via FedEx International Priority. Chinese domestic clients are quoted in CNY and should request a separate domestic price sheet.

Rush orders (where available) require written confirmation from our customer service team before the surcharge is applied. We will not apply a rush surcharge without explicit client agreement. Rush orders cannot be cancelled once production has begun.

For cases involving unusual tooth morphology, non-standard shades, or patient-specific technical requirements, we recommend submitting a pre-case consultation request before sending the physical impression. This avoids remakes and ensures the most accurate pricing.
