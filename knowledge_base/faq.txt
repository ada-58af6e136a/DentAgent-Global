# ── PRICING ─────────────────────────────────────────────────

Q: What is the price for a full-ceramic zirconia crown?
A: A full-ceramic zirconia crown is priced at approximately USD 45 to USD 65 per unit, depending on case complexity and the specific zirconia grade selected. Standard monolithic zirconia is at the lower end of this range. High-translucency layered zirconia, which offers superior aesthetics, is priced toward the upper end. The standard lead time is 5 to 7 business days from receipt of a clean impression. Minimum order quantity is 1 unit.

Q: What is the price for a PFM crown?
A: PFM (porcelain fused to metal) crowns are priced at approximately USD 30 to USD 45 per unit. This includes the metal substructure and porcelain layering. The standard lead time is 5 to 7 business days. PFM is one of our most cost-effective options and has a strong clinical track record for posterior restorations.

Q: What is the price for an implant crown on a titanium base?
A: An implant crown on a titanium base (Ti-base abutment crown) is priced at approximately USD 75 to USD 110 per unit, depending on the implant system and crown material. Please provide the implant brand, platform diameter, and connection type when requesting a quotation so we can confirm compatibility. Standard lead time is 7 business days.

Q: What is the price for a complete denture?
A: A complete denture for a single arch is priced at approximately USD 120 to USD 180, depending on the acrylic grade and teeth selection. A full set covering both upper and lower arches is approximately USD 220 to USD 320. Standard lead time is 7 to 10 business days from receipt of the master model and bite registration.

Q: What is the price for a partial removable denture?
A: A partial removable denture with a cobalt-chromium metal framework is priced at approximately USD 85 to USD 130, depending on the number of clasps and teeth. An acrylic partial denture without a metal framework is priced at approximately USD 50 to USD 80. Standard lead time is 7 business days.

Q: What is the price for a dental veneer?
A: Porcelain veneers are priced at approximately USD 40 to USD 60 per unit. E.max pressed veneers are priced at approximately USD 55 to USD 75 per unit due to the superior translucency and aesthetics of the material. Standard lead time is 5 to 7 business days.

Q: What is the price for an inlay or onlay?
A: Zirconia inlays and onlays are priced at approximately USD 35 to USD 55 per unit. E.max pressed inlays and onlays are priced at approximately USD 45 to USD 65 per unit. Standard lead time is 5 business days.

Q: Do you offer a rush or express production option?
A: Yes, we offer a 3 business day rush production option for most crown and bridge cases. The rush surcharge is 30% of the standard unit price. Rush orders must be confirmed before 10:00 am GMT+8 to begin production the same day. Rush availability is subject to current production capacity, so please contact us to confirm before scheduling a patient appointment around a rush delivery.

Q: Is there a minimum order quantity?
A: There is no minimum order quantity. We accept single-unit orders. For orders of 10 units or more placed in a single submission, we offer a 5% volume discount on the unit price.

Q: Do you offer a formal quotation document?
A: Yes. We provide a formal PDF quotation for any case or product inquiry. Please send us the case details including product type, material, quantity, and any special requirements, and we will return a formal quotation within 1 business day.

Q: Do your prices include shipping?
A: Our listed prices do not include international shipping. Shipping costs depend on the destination country, package weight, and the courier service selected. We will include a shipping estimate in your formal quotation. For regular clients, shipping is typically consolidated and invoiced monthly.

Q: Are your prices in USD and do you accept other currencies?
A: All prices are quoted in USD. We also accept payment in EUR and GBP. For non-USD payments, the mid-market exchange rate on the invoice date is applied. Please contact us if you require invoicing in a specific currency.

Q: Can you send a price list?
A: We can provide a current price list by email on request. However, because pricing depends on material grade, case complexity, and volume, we recommend requesting a formal quotation for any specific case to ensure the most accurate figure. Please contact us with your product type and material preference and we will respond within 1 business day.

# ── MATERIAL ────────────────────────────────────────────────

Q: What materials do you work with?
A: We work with the following primary materials: monolithic zirconia, high-translucency layered zirconia (including Wieland and Katana brands), e.max lithium disilicate (pressed and CAD), porcelain fused to metal with cobalt-chromium or precious metal alloys, PMMA acrylic resin for temporary restorations, PEEK for implant-supported frameworks, and standard acrylic for removable prosthetics. We can also work with patient-specified brands — please contact us if you need a material not listed here.

Q: What material do you recommend for a patient with bruxism?
A: For patients with bruxism, we strongly recommend monolithic zirconia. It has a flexural strength of approximately 900 to 1200 MPa, making it highly resistant to fracture under heavy occlusal load. We do not recommend layered ceramic, e.max, or feldspathic porcelain for bruxism cases, as these materials are significantly more prone to chipping under repeated high-force contact. If aesthetics are important for anterior teeth, high-translucency monolithic zirconia offers a good compromise between strength and appearance.

Q: What is the difference between monolithic zirconia and layered zirconia?
A: Monolithic zirconia is milled from a single zirconia block with no separate porcelain layer. It is extremely strong at 900 to 1200 MPa, highly resistant to chipping, and the most cost-effective zirconia option. Translucency is moderate, making it best suited to posterior teeth. Layered zirconia uses a zirconia coping with hand-applied porcelain on the visible surfaces. It offers superior aesthetics and better light transmission, making it the preferred choice for anterior teeth, but it is more expensive and carries a slightly higher chipping risk under heavy bite forces.

Q: What is the difference between zirconia and PFM?
A: Zirconia crowns are completely metal-free, have no dark metal margin line at the gumline, offer better biocompatibility, and look more natural under various lighting conditions. PFM crowns have a metal substructure that can create a visible gray margin over time as the gum recedes, but they are more cost-effective and have a very long clinical track record. For anterior teeth or patients with aesthetic sensitivity, zirconia is generally preferred. For posterior teeth with heavy bite forces or budget constraints, PFM remains a reliable and proven option.

Q: What is e.max and when should I use it?
A: E.max is a lithium disilicate ceramic material known for exceptional aesthetics, excellent translucency, and a natural appearance that closely mimics natural tooth structure. Its flexural strength is approximately 360 to 400 MPa. E.max is ideal for anterior crowns, veneers, inlays, and onlays where appearance is the primary clinical priority. It is not recommended for patients with bruxism or for high-load posterior restorations. Both e.max pressed and e.max CAD are available — pressed generally delivers slightly better aesthetics for veneers.

Q: What material do you recommend for anterior teeth where aesthetics are the priority?
A: For anterior teeth where aesthetics are the primary concern, we recommend either high-translucency layered zirconia or e.max lithium disilicate. High-translucency zirconia offers better durability, while e.max offers the most natural light transmission and is preferred for patients who are highly aesthetic-conscious. For veneers specifically, e.max pressed is typically our first recommendation.

Q: What material do you recommend for posterior teeth?
A: For posterior teeth, we recommend monolithic zirconia as the first choice. It offers the best balance of strength, durability, and cost for molar and premolar restorations. If aesthetics in the premolar region are important and the patient is not a bruxer, high-translucency monolithic zirconia is a good option. PFM is a reliable and cost-effective alternative for posterior teeth.

Q: What material do you use for temporary crowns?
A: We use PMMA acrylic resin for temporary crowns and bridges. PMMA temporaries can be milled from a CAD design or pressed from a model. They are tooth-coloured, comfortable for the patient, and can be fabricated to match the planned final restoration shape. Standard lead time for PMMA temporaries is 3 to 5 business days.

Q: What is PEEK and when is it used?
A: PEEK is a high-performance polymer used for implant-supported frameworks, full-arch implant bridges, and some removable prosthetic bases. It is completely metal-free, very lightweight, and has excellent biocompatibility. PEEK is used as an alternative to titanium for implant frameworks in patients with metal sensitivities, and for full-arch implant restorations where weight reduction is a clinical priority.

Q: Which zirconia brands do you work with?
A: We work with leading zirconia brands including Wieland Zenostar and Kuraray Noritake Katana, both of which meet ISO 13356 standards. If you have a preference for a specific brand, please specify it in your case instructions. If no brand is specified, we will select the most appropriate grade based on the restoration type and clinical requirements.

Q: Can you match a specific VITA shade?
A: Yes. We use the VITA Classical shade guide as our standard reference and can also match VITA 3D-Master shades. Please specify the shade code clearly in your case prescription, for example A2, B1, or 2M2, and indicate which shade system you are using. For complex shade matching or cases adjacent to natural teeth with unusual translucency, we recommend sending a photograph of the patient's teeth alongside the shade tab for reference.

Q: What is the Wieland zirconia material and what are its properties?
A: Wieland Zenostar is a premium multilayer zirconia from Wieland Dental, known for its natural color gradient from a more opaque cervical zone to a translucent incisal zone. It is well-suited to anterior crowns and bridges where both strength and aesthetics are required. Flexural strength is approximately 700 to 900 MPa depending on the grade. Wieland zirconia is available in standard and high-translucency versions and is one of our most frequently specified materials for aesthetic anterior cases.

# ── ORDER PROGRESS ──────────────────────────────────────────

Q: How long does production take for a standard crown or bridge?
A: The standard production lead time for crown and bridge cases in zirconia, PFM, or e.max is 5 to 7 business days from the date we receive a clean, usable impression or digital scan file. Removable prosthetics such as complete dentures and partial dentures have a standard lead time of 7 to 10 business days. Rush production in 3 business days is available for most crown and bridge cases at a 30% surcharge.

Q: What are the production stages my order goes through?
A: Once we receive your case, it passes through the following stages in sequence. First, incoming inspection, where we verify the impression or scan file quality and check the prescription for completeness. Second, CAD design, where our technicians design the restoration digitally. Third, milling or pressing, where the restoration is fabricated from the selected material. Fourth, porcelain application for layered restorations, where ceramic is applied and fired in multiple stages. Fifth, finishing and polishing, where the restoration is refined and adjusted for occlusion. Sixth, quality control, where a senior technician inspects the final restoration against the original prescription. Seventh, dispatch, where the case is packaged and shipped with a tracking number.

Q: How will I know when my order has shipped?
A: We send a dispatch notification by email when your order leaves our facility. The notification includes the tracking number, the courier service used, and the estimated delivery date. If you do not receive a dispatch notification by the expected date, please contact us with your order reference number and we will investigate immediately.

Q: My order has not arrived and the expected date has passed — what should I do?
A: Please first check the tracking number from your dispatch notification on the courier's website. If the tracking shows no movement for more than 3 consecutive business days, or if the package is significantly overdue, please contact us with your order reference number. We will contact the courier directly and provide you with an update within 1 business day.

Q: What can cause a delay in my order?
A: The most common causes of production delays are: an impression that is not clear or complete enough to design from, in which case we will contact you immediately to request a remake; a prescription that is missing the shade, material, or restoration type; a design revision requested after production has already started; or a specific material or brand being temporarily out of stock. We will always notify you as soon as a delay is identified and provide a revised expected completion date.

Q: How do I know you have received my impression?
A: We send an email acknowledgement within 1 business day of receiving your case package. The acknowledgement confirms receipt, includes the case reference number we have assigned, and states the expected dispatch date. If you do not receive an acknowledgement within 2 business days of sending your package, please contact us with your shipping tracking number so we can locate it.

Q: Can I request a status update on my order at any time?
A: Yes. Please email us with your order reference number at any time and we will provide a current production status update within a few hours during business hours (Monday to Saturday, 9 am to 6 pm GMT+8). We aim to respond to all status enquiries on the same business day.

# ── TECHNICAL ───────────────────────────────────────────────

Q: What happens if the impression I sent is not usable?
A: If we receive an impression that is unclear, incomplete, torn, or damaged, we will contact you within 24 hours of receiving the package and describe the specific problem. We will ask you to send a new impression at your earliest convenience. Once we receive the corrected impression, production begins immediately and the standard lead time applies from that date. There is no additional charge for the first impression quality notification.

Q: Can I send a digital scan file instead of a physical impression?
A: Yes. We accept digital impressions in STL format from all major intraoral scanners including 3Shape Trios, Carestream CS 3600, iTero, Cerec Primescan, and Planmeca Emerald. The submission should include the preparation scan, the opposing arch scan, and a bite registration scan. Send the files by email or a file-sharing link. For files larger than 25 MB, please use WeTransfer, Google Drive, or Dropbox rather than email attachment.

Q: What file format and resolution is required for digital scans?
A: We accept STL files in both ASCII and binary format. The scan resolution should clearly capture the preparation margin line — a point spacing of 50 microns or finer is recommended for crown and bridge preparations. If the margin in the scan is not clearly distinguishable, we will contact you before production begins to request a rescan. Please also include a prescription note specifying the tooth number, restoration type, material, and shade.

Q: We made an error in the prescription — can it be corrected?
A: If you notice a prescription error before production has started, please contact us immediately and we will update the case at no charge. If production has already started, the ability to correct depends on the stage reached. Minor changes such as a shade adjustment before porcelain firing can often be accommodated. Major changes such as a material switch may require restarting the case. Please contact us as soon as possible and we will advise on the impact and options.

Q: The finished restoration does not match the design we requested — what should we do?
A: Please contact us with your order reference number, a clear photograph of the restoration, and a description of the discrepancy compared to your original prescription. Our technical team will review the case against the prescription records and respond within 2 business days with a proposed resolution. Please do not adjust or modify the restoration before contacting us, as this may affect our ability to assess the issue.

Q: What bite registration do you require?
A: For crown and bridge cases, we require a clear bite registration showing the occlusal relationship between the upper and lower arches. We accept wax bite records, silicone bite registration material, or a digital bite scan. An accurate bite registration is the single most important factor in achieving correct occlusion and reducing the need for chairside adjustment. If you are sending a physical impression, please include the bite record in the same package and label it clearly.

Q: Can I request a specific occlusal design or contact scheme?
A: Yes. Please specify any occlusal design preferences in your case notes, for example cuspid-protected occlusion, group function, or specific contact requirements. Our technicians will design to your specification. If no occlusal scheme is specified, we will use the standard protocol appropriate for the restoration type and location.

# ── REWORK ──────────────────────────────────────────────────

Q: The crown does not seat fully or has an open margin — what should I do?
A: Please contact us with your order reference number, a description of the fit issue such as open margin, rocking, or inability to fully seat, and photographs if available. A silicone fit test result is also very helpful if you have one. Our quality team will review the case records and respond within 2 business hours with a proposed resolution. Please do not grind or adjust the internal surface of the restoration before contacting us, as this affects our ability to assess the source of the fit issue.

Q: There is a chip or fracture in the porcelain — what should I do?
A: Please stop using the restoration and contact us with your order reference number, the location of the chip or fracture, and photographs. Please also tell us when the restoration was delivered, when it was fitted, and any relevant clinical information such as whether the patient has heavy bite forces or parafunctional habits. Our quality team will determine whether the damage is a production issue or a clinical factor and respond within 2 business hours with next steps.

Q: The shade of the restoration does not match what we ordered — what can be done?
A: Please contact us with your order reference number, a photograph of the restoration held next to the shade tab specified in the original prescription, and a photograph of the restoration in the patient's mouth next to adjacent teeth if possible. Note the lighting conditions in the photographs. Our team will assess whether the shade is within accepted clinical tolerance and respond within 2 business hours. If a remake is required, it will be prioritised in our production queue.

Q: How long does a rework or remake take?
A: Once a rework is confirmed and approved by our quality team, the standard production lead time applies — 5 to 7 business days for crown and bridge cases. If the patient has an urgent appointment, please inform us and we will assess whether 3-day rush production is possible for the rework. Approved rework cases are given priority in the production schedule.

Q: What information do I need to provide when reporting a quality issue?
A: To process your report as quickly as possible, please provide: your order reference number; the tooth number and restoration type; a clear description of the issue such as fit, shade, fracture, or design discrepancy; photographs of the restoration both isolated and in the patient's mouth where possible; the date the restoration was delivered and fitted; and any relevant clinical details such as the patient's bite force, opposing material, cement type used, or any chairside adjustments made before the issue was identified.

# ── BILLING ─────────────────────────────────────────────────

Q: What are your standard payment terms?
A: Our standard payment terms are net 30 days from the invoice date for established accounts. New clients are asked to prepay for their first three orders before credit terms are extended. We also offer a prepaid account option where a balance is deposited in advance and orders are deducted as they are completed — this is popular with high-volume clients as it simplifies monthly reconciliation and avoids per-order payments.

Q: How do I receive my monthly invoice or processing statement?
A: We send a consolidated monthly invoice and itemised processing statement by email on the first business day of each month, covering all orders dispatched in the previous month. Each line item shows the case reference number, tooth number, restoration type, material, unit price, and any applicable surcharges. If you need a copy of a previous monthly statement, please contact us with the month required and we will resend within 1 business day.

Q: I think there is an error on my invoice — what should I do?
A: Please contact us with your invoice number, the specific line item you believe is incorrect, and the reason for the discrepancy. Our accounts team will cross-reference against the case records and respond within 2 business days. If an error is confirmed, a corrected invoice will be issued promptly and any credit due will be applied to your next statement.

Q: Do you provide itemised invoices?
A: Yes. Every invoice is fully itemised by case, showing the case reference number, tooth number, product type, material selected, unit price, rush surcharge if applicable, and volume discount if applicable. Shipping costs are listed as a separate line item at the bottom of the invoice. We do not issue consolidated lump-sum invoices.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept international bank transfer via SWIFT or IBAN, PayPal for amounts under USD 500, and credit card payment via our secure payment link for amounts under USD 1000. Bank transfer is our preferred method for regular monthly settlements. Please include your invoice number as the payment reference so our accounts team can match your payment to your account without delay.

Q: Can I get a credit note if a case was remade due to a production error?
A: Yes. If a rework or remake is approved by our quality team as a confirmed production error on our part, a credit note for the original case value will be issued. The credit note is applied to your next monthly invoice. Credits are processed after the rework is completed and the quality determination has been made. Please contact us once the rework is finished to request the credit note if you have not received it automatically.

Q: How do I update my billing address or account details?
A: Please send us your updated details by email and we will update your account within 1 business day. Updated billing information will be reflected on your next monthly invoice. If you need a corrected invoice for a previous month with your updated details, please let us know and we will reissue it.
